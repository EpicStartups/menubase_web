Your staff was never going to upsell.

It's not that they're lazy. It's not that they don't care about your restaurant. The system was always going to fail at the upsell job - and trying to fix it with training, scripts, and "smile more" pep talks has never worked. Anywhere.

Language barriers

Your foreign staff can take orders. They can't explain your specials, your story, or why your new dish is worth ordering - in the customer's language.

Zero incentive to upsell

They get paid hourly to clock in. Pushing your RM14 cheesecake doesn't show up in their paycheck. So they don't push it.

Transient workforce

By the time you've trained them on the menu, the specials, the upsell scripts - they've already moved to a different job. Then you start over.

Static menus, static results

Your menu doesn't know it's 9pm and you have 4 lava cakes left. It doesn't know it's Friday. It doesn't know the new arrival ordered last week's special and might like the new one.

This is not a staff problem. It's a system problem. Stop blaming your team for not doing the impossible. Move the upsell job to the only thing that can actually do it reliably - the screen.
FEATURES

The smartest waiter does three things.

Three jobs that humans struggle to do reliably. Three jobs MenuBase does on every order, in every language, every shift.

01 / SMART UPSELL

Suggests the right thing. At the right moment.

No random popups. No tablet that asks "would you like fries with that". MenuBase reads the basket, reads the time, reads what's converted before, and suggests one thing with a real chance of being accepted.

  • Context-aware: 9:42am with avo toast suggests White Coffee. 6pm Friday with pasta suggests Truffle Fries.

  • Self-learning: Suggestions improve on every order, without you writing a single rule.
02 / SMART PROMOTE

Launch a special at 9am. Selling by 9:01.

No printed table tents. No "tell every staff member about the new dessert" briefings. Flag an item as "push this week" and it appears in the suggestion slot on every relevant order across every outlet, instantly.

  • One-click rollout: Push a new special across all outlets, all shifts, all languages, instantly.
  • Happy hour automation: Specials flip on at 5pm. Off at 7pm. Without anyone touching a thing.
  • Move slow stock: Auto-surface items that haven't moved, paired with baskets where they'd fit.
03 / DYNAMIC MENU

The menu that reads the room.

Your customer should never see "sorry, not available" again. Your breakfast menu shouldn't sell breakfast at 3pm. Your high-margin items should sit at the top when the rush hits.

  • Language-aware: Same menu auto-translates into your customer's language, they pick with one tap.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Things operators ask.

Do I need to replace my POS?
No. MenuBase sits on top of whatever you already use, StoreHub, Slurp!, Loyverse, Zeoniq, IRC, anything. Orders come out on a waiter tablet, and the waiter types them into the POS exactly the way they do today. Your billing, sales reports and accounting stay where they are.
How long does setup take?
For founding outlets: same day. We come in with your existing menu PDF, build the digital version in MenuBase, generate your table QR codes and walk your team through the waiter tablet. You're live by end of service.
Do my customers need to download an app?
Never. MenuBase opens in the browser the moment they scan the QR. Works on every smartphone made in the last decade. No login, no signup, no email capture, just the menu and an upsell that doesn't feel like one.
What languages does the AI waiter speak?
English, Bahasa Malaysia, Mandarin and Tamil out of the box. Customer picks once, the whole menu and every suggestion auto-translates. We can add Arabic, Vietnamese, Burmese, Tagalog, most launch-ready within a week.
How does the upsell actually work?
It's a single suggestion shown at the right moment, not random popups. We look at the items already in the basket, the time of day, and what has actually converted on similar baskets before. Then we surface one item. Customer taps to add. No staff awkwardness, no pressure on the customer.
What does it cost?
Founding outlets pay nothing until we prove uplift on their own menu. After that, transparent monthly pricing, locked in for life. We'll cover the numbers on the demo call.
What if my team isn't tech-savvy?
Then MenuBase is built for you. The waiter only ever sees a list of items on a tablet and types it into the POS, the same job they already do. We're explicitly designed so foreign-worker, transient and shift-rotation staff need zero training to operate it.
