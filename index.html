Features
Your Best Server. Every Table. Every Time.
An intelligent ordering layer that sits in front of your POS. It handles the soft sell so your staff doesn't have to.

Context-Aware Suggestions

Analyses time of day, basket contents, and ordering patterns to recommend the right upsell - not a random popup.

QR-Based Ordering

Customers scan and order from their own phone. No app install, no queue, no awkward waiter interactions.

POS-Agnostic

Self-Learning Engine

Tracks what converts and what doesn't. Suggestion quality improves automatically over time without manual rules.
