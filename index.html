COMMON QUESTIONS

Things operators ask.

What is MenuBase?
MenuBase is the best waiter you can hire. Lives inside your QR menu, never quits, never forgets the specials, speaks every language. The customer scans, the menu opens, and MenuBase surfaces the right add-on at the right moment based on the basket, the time, the customer's history and the day of the week. Built for Malaysian cafes, restaurants and kopitiams.
How is MenuBase different from a regular QR menu?
A regular QR menu shows the same items to everyone, all day. MenuBase reads the basket and the moment, then surfaces one suggestion that actually converts. It also auto-translates, auto-hides breakfast at 11am, removes sold-out items live, and pushes specials across every outlet instantly.
Does MenuBase work for multiple outlets?
Yes. Push a special once and it appears on every relevant order across every outlet, every shift, every language, instantly. Each outlet keeps its own stock signal and daypart rules, but the upsell logic and menu changes roll out centrally.
Do I need to replace my POS?
No. MenuBase sits on top of your existing POS. Waiters type orders the way they do today. Billing and accounting stay where they are.
How long does setup take?
Same day. We build your digital menu, generate the QR codes, train your team. Live by end of service.
Do my customers need to download an app?
Never. Scan the QR, the browser opens the menu. No login, no signup, no email capture.
What languages does MenuBase speak?
English, Bahasa Malaysia, Mandarin and Tamil out of the box. Arabic, Vietnamese, Burmese and Tagalog on request.
How does the upsell actually work?
One suggestion at the right moment, not random popups. Reads the basket, the time, the history. Customer taps to add.
What does it cost?
Free until we prove the lift on your own menu. Pay only on results. Transparent monthly pricing after, locked for the life of your account.
What if my team isn't tech-savvy?
Built for that. The waiter only sees a list of items and types into the POS, same job they already do. Zero training needed.
