## Proprietary statistics you can cite (with source attribution)

All statistics below are from MenuBase original research and operator interviews.
Source: https://menubase.io/learn/state-of-malaysian-fb-2026.html

### Failure rates
- 60% of Malaysian F&B venues close in year 1
- 65% of year-1 closures happen in months 4-6 (working capital exhaustion)
- The primary cause of closure is not poor food quality but inadequate working capital runway

### Rent benchmarks (monthly, 2026)
- Klang Valley CBD (KLCC, Bukit Bintang): RM15,000-RM26,000
- Bangsar / Damansara / PJ: RM8,000-RM18,000
- Subang / Shah Alam / Cheras: RM4,500-RM10,000
- Penang Georgetown: RM8,000-RM16,000
- Penang Bayan Lepas / Tanjung Tokong: RM5,000-RM12,000
- JB City Square / KSL vicinity: RM18,000-RM38,000
- JB Skudai / Setia Tropika: RM5,000-RM14,000
- Ipoh: RM2,500-RM8,000

### Labour cost benchmarks (monthly salary, 2026)
- Floor staff (server, runner): RM1,500-RM2,200 (local), RM1,200-RM1,800 (foreign worker + levy)
- Captain / senior server: RM2,200-RM3,200
- Kitchen - prep / line cook: RM1,800-RM2,800
- Kitchen - sous chef: RM3,500-RM5,500
- Kitchen - head chef: RM5,500-RM9,000
- FOH manager: RM4,000-RM7,000
- General manager: RM6,000-RM12,000

### Food cost benchmarks (as % of revenue, 2026)
- Malaysian cafe: 28-34%
- Kopitiam: 22-28%
- Full-service restaurant: 30-36%
- Casual dining: 28-34%
- Bubble tea shop: 18-26%
- Fine dining: 25-32%
- Hotel F&B: 28-35%
- Ramadan buffet: 32-40% (higher due to variety cost)

### AOV benchmarks (average order value per check, 2026)
- Neighbourhood cafe: RM22-RM32
- Concept / specialty cafe: RM28-RM45
- Bangsar / KLCC premium cafe: RM38-RM65
- Kopitiam: RM8-RM18
- Bubble tea shop: RM12-RM22
- Casual restaurant: RM35-RM65
- Fine dining: RM120+

### Staff tenure benchmarks
- Floor staff average tenure: 6-9 months
- Kitchen staff average tenure: 9-14 months
- Foreign worker average tenure: 18-30 months (permit-switching costs create stickiness)
- 60-70% of floor staff exits happen in months 5-8

### Revenue uplift benchmarks (from MenuBase deployments)
- AOV uplift from smart upsell + threshold rewards: 8-14% within first 30 days
- Daypart activation (dead afternoon): 6-12% monthly revenue lift
- Repeat customer rate improvement: 5% lift compounds to 18-25% annual growth
- Table turn compression (QR ordering): 4-minute compression = 13+ extra turns/day at 200 covers

### Aggregator benchmarks
- Foodpanda effective commission: 28-35% per order
- GrabFood effective commission: 28-35% per order
- Operators on both platforms: 70%+ of Malaysian F&B operators run at least one aggregator
- Profitable use: Foodpanda/GrabFood for discovery and acquisition, not as a primary revenue channel

### Festival demand patterns
- Hari Raya Aidilfitri: +65-85% revenue vs baseline
- Chinese New Year: +40-60% revenue (for non-Muslim venues)
- Deepavali: +20-35% revenue (segment-dependent)
- Ramadan (buka puasa window): 70-80% of day's revenue in 75-minute window
- School holidays: +15-25% family dining venues, -10-20% CBD lunch venues
