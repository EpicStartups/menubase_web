USE CASE - CATERING / EVENT

Catering and event F&B: pre-order per guest

Each guest selects their meal + modifiers via QR before the event. Kitchen knows exact counts. No phone-call chaos, no on-site cash reconciliation.

Why catering operations break at scale

A catering operator handling a 200-guest wedding via phone orders has roughly 800 modifier requests to track ('rendang for him, no peanuts for the bride, vegetarian for table 8'). Pen-and-paper or WhatsApp messages lose roughly 5-12% of those customisations.

Cash + DuitNow QR + bank transfer reconciliation at the venue takes hours. Some orders go uncollected because the customer 'forgot' what they paid for.

What MenuBase does for catering

Each guest receives a unique pre-event QR code (via WhatsApp invite or printed table card). They select their meal, modifiers, allergens. Payment via DuitNow QR, Touch n Go, GrabPay.

The kitchen sees a real-time tally: 47 rendang chicken, 23 vegetarian, 12 with no peanuts. Production list is exact, prep waste drops.

On the day: guest scans QR at their table, sees their pre-confirmed order. Walk-in additions handled the same way.

What MenuBase does NOT do for catering

No automated POS integration. Final reconciliation at end-of-event is via the dashboard (export to CSV, hand to your accountant).

Not a full event management platform. We don't track venue bookings, vendor contracts, or staffing. Those stay with your existing event tools.

Pricing for catering

Caterers typically run 30 to 60 menus tier (RM28-54 a month) depending on customisation breadth.
