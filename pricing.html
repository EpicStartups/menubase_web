What's actually included.

We deliberately price flat. The hidden costs that bite Malaysian operators on competing systems are not hidden on us.

Included in every tier

  • Same-day setup (we build your menu, generate QRs, train the team in one shift)
  • Smart upsell on every relevant basket, no script required
  • Promo push that fires the moment you flip a special live
  • Daypart-aware menu (breakfast hides at 11am, lunch combos go live at 11:01)
  • Stock-aware menu rules (sold-out items vanish, "last 3" tags surface)
  • Payment integration with Touch n Go eWallet, GrabPay, Boost, DuitNow QR, FPX, major cards
  • Unlimited menu revisions, no cap on edits per month
  • Full data export to CSV in standard formats, included in the base rate
  • Monthly cancellation, no long-term lock-in

Never charged for

  • Setup or onboarding fees
  • Per-language add-ons (where multilingual is supported, it is in the base rate)
  • Per-menu-revision overage fees
  • Per-payment-method integration fees
  • Sponsored placement, promo participation or other engagement up-charges
  • Data export or contract-exit fees
  • Proprietary hardware lock-in (you use existing terminals and QR table cards)
  • Per-transaction commission on orders (we are not a payment processor)

Pricing questions, answered honestly.

What does "free until the lift hits" actually mean?
You pay zero until the AOV lift shows up in your numbers. Happy hour, threshold rewards, smart upsell and the daypart menu go live on your venue day one. Customers click in. Most venues see the lift within the first week and settle 8 to 14% above their pre-MenuBase AOV by end of month one. Only after that does a monthly bill start. Risk is on us, not you.
What is the monthly cost after the lift hits?
Tiered by venue type. Kopitiams and kiosks sit at the lower end of the Malaysian QR menu market range. Cafes and bubble tea shops sit at the middle tier. Full-service restaurants and multi-outlet chains sit at the upper tier. For exact RM figures for your specific venue and outlet count, WhatsApp the team. We quote in writing within 24 hours. (For the vendor-neutral context of QR menu pricing in Malaysia, see our QR menu pricing primer.)
Is there a setup fee?
No. Setup is included. We come in with your existing menu, build the digital version, generate the QR codes for every table or counter, and walk the team through the system in one shift. By end of service on day one you are live.
Are there per-language or per-revision fees?
No. Multilingual support (where supported) is included in the base rate. Menu revisions are unlimited - no cap on edits per month. We have written separately on how hidden per-language and per-revision fees inflate the real cost of competing systems; we deliberately do not do that.
What about hardware?
QR codes for the tables come included (printed sticker or table tent options). The customer side requires nothing - no app to download, no hardware to install. The waiter tablet is optional; some venues use it for parallel order receiving, others stick with their existing POS workflow. We discuss the hardware decision in the setup conversation.
What if we want to cancel?
Monthly cancellation, no lock-in. Your menu, customer data and historical sales export to CSV in standard formats within 24 hours of request. We do not believe in trapping operators in long contracts; if MenuBase is no longer earning its keep at your venue we want you to be free to switch.
Do we need to replace our POS?
No. MenuBase sits on top of any POS. Billing, accounting, payment processing and sales reports stay where they are. The waiter rings orders into the existing system the same way.
How do we get an actual quote?
WhatsApp the team with your venue type, outlet count, and a photo of your menu. We quote in writing within 24 hours - itemised, no hidden lines, no setup fee, no per-language fee. If MenuBase is not the right fit for your venue, we will say so.
