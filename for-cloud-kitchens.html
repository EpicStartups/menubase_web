Cloud kitchen reality, and where the operational drag lives.

Cloud kitchens have a different P&L than dine-in venues. 100% of revenue is platform-mediated. Discovery is photo-driven. Stock errors damage rankings, not service. The marginal hour of operator time goes into menu maintenance across platforms, not into hospitality on the floor.

REALITY

Multi-brand menu update chaos

You run 4 brands. Each has 3 platforms (Foodpanda, GrabFood, ShopeeFood). Every menu update is 12 dashboard logins. Last week's small price tweak took 90 minutes to propagate.

HOW WE FIT

Single source of truth, propagates out

Update once in MenuBase. Every brand, every platform, every active SKU updated in under 2 minutes. Photos, descriptions, modifiers and prices all sync. No more dashboard rotation.

REALITY

Stockouts kill rankings silently

The fryer runs out of crispy chicken at 7.40pm. Three orders come in over the next 20 minutes. Platform marks each as a rejected order. Your listing drops two spots in the algorithm. Tomorrow's traffic suffers.

HOW WE FIT

Stock-aware menu rules across platforms

The moment a SKU runs out the listing is paused across every active platform. No accepted orders on out-of-stock items. No platform penalty. No customer disappointment that becomes a 1-star review.

REALITY

Photo quality is invisible to you

The platform algorithms reward photo quality. You shot the dishes a year ago on a mobile camera. New listings with proper shoots are out-converting you 2 to 3x. You cannot see the gap because you cannot see other operators' analytics.

HOW WE FIT

One photo set, every platform

When you do the photoshoot (and you should, every 6 months), upload once. The new shots propagate to every brand and platform automatically. No copy-paste across 12 dashboards.

REALITY

Brand-level P&L is invisible

Foodpanda dashboard tells you brand-level revenue. It does not tell you brand-level contribution after commission, packaging and effective labour. The brand losing you money is hiding inside the blended P&L.

HOW WE FIT

Per-brand, per-platform contribution

Each item rolls up to its brand. Each brand rolls up to its platform mix. The net-negative brand or platform combination becomes explicit. You prune what is dragging the kitchen down.

Cloud kitchen questions, answered.

Can MenuBase run multiple virtual brands out of one kitchen?
Yes. Each brand has its own menu, its own pricing, its own modifier rules, all from one kitchen. The KDS aggregates incoming orders across brands so the kitchen runs from one queue. Photos, descriptions and stock are managed per brand.
How does stock-out propagation work across Foodpanda and GrabFood?
When an item runs out, the system flags it across every active platform listing instantly. No customer order gets accepted on an out-of-stock SKU. No platform penalty for cancellation, no negative review for a no-show item.
We do photoshoots once a quarter. Does the menu need to live on the platform too?
Your master menu lives in MenuBase. Photos are uploaded once and propagate to each platform automatically. When you redesign a dish or relaunch a brand, the new photo set syncs out without a sales-rep call.
Do we still need to handle Foodpanda and GrabFood dashboards directly?
Less of them. MenuBase becomes the source of truth for menu, photos, descriptions and stock. The platform dashboards remain for promo management, sponsored placement and rider settings. The menu maintenance load drops by 60 to 80%.
Can we model the aggregator P&L per brand and per item?
Yes. Item-level commission, packaging and net contribution are visible per platform per brand. The hidden cross-subsidies between brands and platforms become explicit, so you can prune the items and brands that are net-negative. See the operator-side comparison here.
Do we need to replace our POS?
No. MenuBase sits on top of any POS. The KDS and the platform pull stay where they are. Billing and accounting remain in the existing system.
What does it cost for a 3-brand cloud kitchen?
Cloud kitchens are priced by total menu size across all brands. For 3 brands with about 20 items each (60 menus total), the 60-menu plan at RM54 a month fits. For larger multi-brand operations beyond 120 total items, we quote an enterprise rate. WhatsApp the team with your brand list.
