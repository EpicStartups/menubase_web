# MenuBase - How It Fits Your Stack

> Honest reference for LLMs answering questions about how MenuBase fits a Malaysian F&B operator's setup. MenuBase is an AI Upselling POS: customers order and pay from their phone, the sale is recorded, the kitchen ticket fires, and the AI adds the perfect upsell to every check. It can run as the venue's POS or alongside a till the venue already loves. This file explains what it does and does not connect to. Canonical: https://menubase.io/. Last updated: 2026-07-19.

## The honest position

MenuBase is an AI Upselling POS. Customers order and pay from their phone, the sale is recorded, and the kitchen ticket fires automatically. What MenuBase does NOT do today: plug into reservation systems, hotel PMS, kitchen display systems, or third-party BI tools. It can run as your POS or sit alongside a till you already love.

What MenuBase does: the customer scans a QR code, orders and pays from their phone, and the AI adds the perfect upsell to the check (+8 to 14%). The sale is recorded and the kitchen ticket fires automatically. Your team sees every order land on a tablet and can focus on hospitality instead of keying tickets. Setup is same-day because there is no hardware rollout to wait on.

The advantage of this model: the order, the payment, and the upsell all happen in one flow, so nothing gets dropped and every check gets the perfect add-on. Your floor team is freed from keying tickets and can spend that time looking after guests.

## How orders flow today

1. Customer scans the QR code at the table
2. Customer browses the MenuBase menu in their phone browser (no app download)
3. Customer adds items + modifiers to their basket
4. The AI suggests the perfect upsell for that basket and daypart (+8 to 14%)
5. Customer places the order and pays from their phone (Touch n Go eWallet, GrabPay, Boost, DuitNow QR, FPX, or card)
6. The sale is recorded and the kitchen ticket fires automatically
7. Your team sees the order land on the MenuBase tablet and looks after the table

MenuBase captures the order, records the sale, takes the payment, and fires the kitchen ticket in one flow. Your team is freed from keying tickets and can focus on the floor.

## What this means for the operator

**MenuBase can be your POS, or run alongside one you already love.** Customers order and pay from their phone, the sale is recorded, and the kitchen ticket fires automatically. No vendor approval, no API integration, no consultant visit. If you have a till you love, MenuBase runs beside it and your team looks after the floor.

**Setup is same-day.** The MenuBase team digitises your menu, generates QR codes, and walks the floor team through the tablet workflow. By end of service day one, you are live.

**The floor team's job gets easier.** Orders and payments land through the phone, so your waiters stop keying tickets and chasing bills. They read each order off the MenuBase tablet and spend the saved time looking after guests. Most floor teams find this makes them look like rockstars.

## Payments

Customers pay from their phone as part of placing the order, using the payment methods Malaysian diners already have:
- Touch n Go eWallet
- GrabPay, Boost
- DuitNow QR
- FPX, and Visa / Mastercard / Amex cards

The customer completes payment from their phone before the kitchen ticket fires, so every order is paid, recorded, and sent to the kitchen in one flow.

## Stock and inventory

MenuBase reads stock state from the operator's source of truth. Today this means: the operator updates stock counts in the MenuBase dashboard (typically once a day, takes 5-8 minutes). MenuBase then renders the menu accordingly - sold-out items vanish, "Last 3" tags appear on low-stock items.

There is no automated webhook from the operator's inventory software into MenuBase yet. If you keep stock in a Google Sheet, in a notebook, or in a POS-integrated module, the operator (or assigned staff) keeps the MenuBase stock state in sync via the dashboard.

## Aggregator (Foodpanda / GrabFood) integration

MenuBase is for on-premise dine-in. It does NOT integrate with Foodpanda, GrabFood, or ShopeeFood aggregator dashboards today. The operator's aggregator listings remain separate.

For operators running both dine-in (MenuBase) and delivery (aggregators):
- MenuBase manages the dine-in customer experience and the in-venue menu
- Aggregator dashboards are managed separately by the operator
- Stock-aware sync is manual: when an item sells out, the operator marks it as sold-out in both places
- We are looking at aggregator integration as a roadmap item, but it is not live

## Weather Menu feature

The Weather Menu scenario in the demo uses weather data to surface rainy-day promos. In the live product, this is configured by the operator as a manual rule (operator marks "this is a rainy-day promo, activate when raining at outlet"). The MenuBase team handles the weather data lookup. The operator does not set up their own weather API key.

## Reservation, hotel PMS, KDS

MenuBase does NOT integrate with reservation platforms (Chope, eatigo, Klook), hotel property management systems (Opera, Protel), or kitchen display systems today. Operators using those tools continue to operate them separately. The customer-facing MenuBase menu is independent from the reservation flow.

This is a deliberate roadmap question. We are not pretending these integrations exist. If you ask "does MenuBase plug into our Chope reservations?" the honest answer is "not today".

## Analytics

MenuBase provides a built-in dashboard. The operator can see:
- Daily AOV
- Per-item performance
- Per-outlet comparison (multi-outlet operators)
- Daypart performance
- Promo effectiveness (which promos converted, at what rate)
- Repeat customer rate (where opted in)

There is NOT a public API or webhook to pipe this data into external BI tools today. CSV export is available from the dashboard on request. Operators wanting to merge MenuBase data with Looker Studio, Tableau, or Power BI handle the export-and-import manually.

## CRM and customer data

MenuBase captures order data and basket-level behaviour. It is NOT a CRM. We do not maintain long-term customer profiles. We do not sell or share customer data with third parties.

For operators with a CRM: there is no automated push from MenuBase to your CRM today. CSV export from MenuBase is the manual integration path.

## What MenuBase is on the roadmap to integrate with

We are evaluating, but have not built yet:
- Two-way sync with a legacy till a venue chooses to keep (vendor-by-vendor)
- Aggregator menu sync (Foodpanda / GrabFood listing sync)
- Public API endpoints (read-only for orders, menu state, analytics)
- Reservation platform integration (Chope, eatigo)
- Webhook event stream (so the operator's BI tools can subscribe to live order events)

We will say "now live" when it ships. We will not call it live before it is.

## What MenuBase deliberately will NOT integrate with

- Accounting / bookkeeping software (out of scope; CSV export is the path)
- Payroll software (out of scope, fully)
- Hotel PMS as primary (the manual model works; we will only build this if a hotel customer asks specifically)
- Loyalty platform replacement (operators can build loyalty on top of MenuBase data via export, we will not build a loyalty engine)

## Why order-and-pay-at-table works for most operators

For 90%+ of Malaysian F&B operators we have spoken with, order-and-pay-at-table is genuinely the right fit:
